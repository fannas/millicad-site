Mad Cad: terms of use, privacy and refunds

Version 1, 11 November 2026. Plain words on purpose. By creating a Mad Cad account you agree to these terms.

1. Who we are

Mad Cad is run by Polytimos, Midrand, South Africa ("we", "us"). Contact: info@fanna.org.

2. What Mad Cad is

Mad Cad is a small program on your own computer that lets an AI assistant help with CAD work in your own CAD program (FreeCAD, Autodesk Fusion, KiCad or SOLIDWORKS). It reads the drawings and files you give it, builds or checks parts, and runs scripts and programs on your computer, but only inside the job folder you choose and only after you have allowed each run (unless you switch that asking off).

Mad Cad is a tool, not an engineer. The AI can be wrong. You check every size, every part and every board before you cut, print, order or build anything. We are not responsible for scrap, wrong parts, lost time or damage that comes from using what Mad Cad produced.

3. Your account

You need an e-mail address and a password. Keep the password to yourself; what happens under your sign-in is yours. One account per person or company. We may close an account that is used to abuse the service, to break the law, or to attack us or the AI provider.

4. Credit, prices and payment

Mad Cad runs on prepaid credit. Each answer uses credit according to how much the AI had to read and write; the amount is shown after every answer and in Settings. Prices are in South African rand when you buy, held as a US dollar balance, and can change at any time; a change never affects credit you already paid for. Prices exclude VAT unless we say otherwise. New accounts get a small free starting credit.

Card payments are handled by Paystack. We never see or store your card number.

5. Refunds

Credit you have not used can be refunded on request within 14 days of the purchase, less the payment provider's fee. Credit that was used cannot be refunded, because the AI work has been paid for by then. Free starting credit has no cash value. Unused paid credit stays valid for 12 months after purchase. To ask for a refund, e-mail us from the address of your account.

6. Your own CAD licences

FreeCAD and KiCad are free. Autodesk Fusion and SOLIDWORKS need your own licence that allows the work you do with them. Mad Cad never installs, unlocks or activates a paid CAD program, and whether your licence allows commercial use is your responsibility.

7. Privacy: what leaves your computer

  • What you type, the files Mad Cad reads for the job (text, pictures of drawings), and the results of scripts it runs are sent to the AI provider (Anthropic) through our server, so the AI can answer. The AI provider's own terms do not allow it to train on this data under the business terms we use.
  • Our server stores: your e-mail address, your credit balance and the amounts used, token counts per answer, and payment references. It does not store your chats, your drawings or your files.
  • Your chats are saved on your own computer only (in the Mad Cad folder under your Windows profile) so you can look back at them.
  • We follow the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and its data, by e-mail. We delete everything except what tax law requires us to keep about payments.
  • We do not sell or share your details with anyone except the AI provider and the payment provider as described above.

8. Fair use

Do not use Mad Cad to design weapons, to break the law, to attack computers or networks, or to produce designs that infringe someone else's rights. Do not try to extract Mad Cad's internal instructions or to use the service in a way that costs us more than you pay, such as automated bulk use. We may set daily limits.

9. Availability and changes

We try to keep Mad Cad running, but it depends on the AI provider, the payment provider and the internet, so we cannot promise it is always available. We may update the program and these terms. If a change matters to you, we tell you in the program before it applies. If you do not agree, stop using Mad Cad and ask for a refund of unused paid credit.

10. Liability

To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you for anything to do with Mad Cad is limited to the amount you paid us in the three months before the problem. Nothing here takes away rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act that cannot be excluded.
