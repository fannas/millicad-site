An AI assistant that works inside your own CAD program

Milli Cad is a small panel that stays on top of your screen. Give it a drawing, a photo or a sketch with sizes, tell it what you need, and it reads, builds and checks the part in FreeCAD, F3D CAD, KiCad or SLDPRT CAD on your PC, inside one job folder you choose.

What it does

Reads drawings properly

Renders the PDF, measures the drawn lines against the printed dimensions, reads the title block, and asks when a size is missing instead of guessing.

Builds in your CAD program

Writes and runs the scripts that create the part, the sheet, the assembly or the circuit board, then checks the result and fixes its own mistakes.

Checks before you cut

Reports sizes, mass, clearances and design-rule results in plain words with tick boxes, so you can confirm before anything is printed, cut or ordered.

Knows the traps

It carries a knowledge pack of real workshop lessons: profile tables, scan scales, thread and hole rules, sheet-metal bends, PCB routing habits.

Stays in its lane

It can only read and write inside the job folder you pick, and it asks before it runs a script or program. You can switch the asking off per chat.

Your files stay with you

Your drawings never leave your PC except for the parts the AI must read to do the job. The server keeps no chats and no files.

How it works

  1. Download the panel. One small program, MilliCad.exe, from the download section. No install wizard, no admin rights. Create an account with your e-mail.
  2. Pick your CAD program and a job folder. Milli Cad checks which programs it can find on your PC and tells you where to get the free ones.
  3. Add your drawing and say what you need. Click + Add or drop the files on the panel. Then: "build this bracket", "check this board", "give me a cut list".
  4. Allow, answer, confirm. Yellow cards ask before a script runs, green cards ask you the questions only you can answer. The result lands in your job folder and in your CAD program.

Privacy and safety

What leaves your PC

What you type, the files Milli Cad reads for the job, and the results of the scripts it runs, sent to the AI provider through our server so the AI can answer. Under the business terms we use, the AI provider does not train on it.

What the server keeps

Your e-mail address, your credit balance and usage, token counts per answer, and payment references. Not your chats, not your drawings, not your files.

Your CAD licence

FreeCAD and KiCad are free. F3D CAD (.f3d) and SLDPRT CAD (.sldprt) need your own licence. Milli Cad never installs, unlocks or activates a paid CAD program.

A tool, not an engineer

The AI can be wrong. Milli Cad shows its work and asks for confirmation, and you check every size before you cut, print or order.
